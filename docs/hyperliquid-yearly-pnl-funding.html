What was my Hyperliquid PnL this year? Funding, fees and realized PnL by year

Hyperliquid's UI shows PnL per position and a portfolio chart, but not a per-year breakdown of realized PnL, fees and funding, which is what you (or your accountant) need at tax time.

What you get

LineWhere it comes from
Perp realized PnLSum of closedPnl on every perp fill closed in the year
Trading feesSum of fee on perp fills (negative = maker rebates)
Funding netSum of hourly funding payments (userFunding); positive means you received more than you paid
Net perp resultRealized PnL − fees + funding
Deposits / withdrawalsUSDC bridged in from and out to Arbitrum
Fill countsPerp and spot fills per year

Things to know

  • Funding is not in your PnL. Hyperliquid's position PnL excludes funding, which can be large for positions held for weeks. Many traders are surprised by the funding line.
  • 10,000-fill window. Hyperliquid's API only returns your latest 10,000 fills. If you trade more than that, the page shows the date your fill data starts. Funding and transfers are complete regardless.
  • Sub-accounts and vaults have their own addresses. Run each one.
  • Read-only: it uses public data for the address you paste. No wallet connection, no signature.
